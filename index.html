How I work

A trusted metric is a system, not a number.

Business question → metric definition → warehouse logic → QA → BI experience. Each step makes the next one more reliable.

  1. 01

    Define

    Write the metric contract: owner, grain, rules, and the decision it supports.

  2. 02

    Model

    Make reusable logic versioned, reviewable, and available beyond one report.

  3. 03

    Validate

    Use source parity, structural checks, and stakeholder review before release.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Expose a governed answer where people make decisions.

About

From maps to metrics.

I studied geography at UMass Amherst, and I still approach data the way a cartographer approaches a map: it should be accurate, honest about what it leaves out, and readable by someone who was not in the room.

My work moved from client data implementations to revenue analytics, geospatial systems, and analytics engineering. The common thread is understanding complicated systems and making them understandable enough to act on.

I use AI coding tools to move faster, and I verify every result before it ships.
